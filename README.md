# LeadPilot AI CRM — Enterprise AI Sales Platform (v3.1.0)

LeadPilot AI CRM is a next-generation autonomous enterprise AI Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform built for high-performance sales, real estate, and enterprise organizations.

---

## AI Sales Copilot Capabilities (v3.1.0)

1. **AI Lead Summary Engine:** Summarizes prospect history, pre-qualified facts, risk indicators, and cross-sell opportunities.
2. **AI Email Assistant:** Generates follow-up email drafts, rewrites messages across tones (`professional`, `persuasive`, `concise`, `friendly`), and synthesizes email threads.
3. **AI WhatsApp Assistant:** Drafts instant replies, schedules follow-up prompts, and summarizes chat logs.
4. **Meeting Preparation Engine:** Compiles pre-meeting executive overviews, interaction timelines, open tasks, and recommended talking points.
5. **Daily Morning AI Brief:** Ranks high-priority leads, flags deals at risk, lists tasks due today, and recommends next best actions.
6. **Deal Health Predictor:** Calculates 0-100 closing probability, health grade (A-F), missing document flags, and risk indicators.
7. **AI Command Center Dashboard:** Consolidated UI (`/copilot`) featuring real-time brief cards, deal health predictions grid, instant action tools, and interactive copilot chat.
