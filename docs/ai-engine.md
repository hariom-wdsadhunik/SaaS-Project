# LeadPilot AI CRM — AI Intelligence Engine Architecture

**Module:** Centralized AI Intelligence Platform  
**Version:** v3.7.0  

---

## 1. Architecture Overview

Sprint **v3.7.0** transforms LeadPilot from an AI-assisted CRM into an AI-driven CRM through a centralized predictive machine learning engine (`AiIntelligenceEngine.ts`).

---

## 2. Platform Core Components

- **Predictive Lead & Opportunity Scoring:** Machine learning model evaluating engagement velocity, activity, communication frequency, response times, and stage alignment.
- **Revenue & Pipeline Forecasting:** Monte Carlo simulation engine projecting monthly, quarterly, and annual revenue with best/worst case bounds and confidence intervals.
- **Next Best Action Engine:** Prescriptive recommendation engine prioritizing sales follow-ups based on expected revenue impact.
- **Explainable AI (XAI):** Feature importance breakdown detailing positive and negative score drivers.
