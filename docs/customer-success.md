## 1. Component Architecture

LeadPilot's Customer Success Platform encompasses:
- **Knowledge Base Help Center:** Article indexing and search.
- **Support Ticket Lifecycle:** Ticket creation, priority escalation, and agent assignment.
- **Customer Health Score Engine:** Composite telemetry calculation (0-100 index).
