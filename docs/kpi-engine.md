# KPI Engine & Metric Calculation Architecture

**Module:** KPI Engine  
**Location:** `src/platform/analytics/`  

---

## 1. Supported Core KPIs

1. **Lead Conversion Rate (`LEAD_CONVERSION_RATE`)**: Percentage of leads converted to qualified status.
2. **Win Rate (`WIN_RATE`)**: Percentage of closed deals won.
3. **Average Deal Value (`AVG_DEAL_VALUE`)**: Mean monetary value across pipeline deals.
4. **Pipeline Value (`PIPELINE_VALUE`)**: Total active opportunity value.
5. **Pipeline Velocity (`PIPELINE_VELOCITY`)**: Estimated daily revenue generation velocity.
6. **Sales Cycle Length (`SALES_CYCLE_LENGTH`)**: Mean days to close deals.
7. **First Response Time (`FIRST_RESPONSE_TIME`)**: Mean minutes to initial lead outreach.
8. **Task Completion Rate (`TASK_COMPLETION_RATE`)**: Percentage of tasks completed.
9. **Appointment Completion Rate (`APPOINTMENT_COMPLETION_RATE`)**: Percentage of walkthroughs completed.
10. **Communication Activity (`COMMUNICATION_ACTIVITY`)**: Total messages dispatched.
11. **Document Activity (`DOCUMENT_ACTIVITY`)**: Total documents and versions stored.
