## 1. Metric Catalog

1. **Revenue:** Gross annual closed volume ($32.2M).
2. **Pipeline Value:** Total active open deal volume ($84.5M).
3. **Deals Won:** YTD count of closed-won transactions (35 Deals).
4. **Deals Lost:** YTD count of lost opportunities.
5. **Lead Conversion Rate:** Percentage of leads converted into closed deals (35.2%).
6. **Average Deal Size:** Mean revenue per closed deal ($920K).
7. **Sales Velocity:** Average cycle duration from lead capture to close (18.4 Days).
8. **Task Completion Rate:** Percentage of assigned tasks completed on time.
9. **Appointment Success Rate:** Ratio of completed vs canceled viewings.
10. **Communication Response Time:** Mean agent response duration.
11. **Workflow Execution Success:** Automated workflow success rate (99.8%).
12. **Customer Growth:** Account expansion rate.
13. **MRR:** Monthly Recurring Revenue ($299K/mo).
14. **ARR:** Annual Recurring Revenue ($3.58M/yr).
15. **Churn:** Monthly account churn rate (1.2%).
16. **Retention:** Net Revenue Retention rate (114.5%).
