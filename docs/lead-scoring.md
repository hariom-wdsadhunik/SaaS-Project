# LeadPilot AI CRM — Predictive Lead & Opportunity Scoring

**Module:** Machine Learning Lead Scoring Model  
**Version:** v3.7.0  

---

## 1. Feature Variables

- **Lead Source:** Inbound organic, referral, direct, or paid campaign.
- **Activity & Interaction Frequency:** Touchpoints logged over past 24h, 7d, 30d.
- **Communication History:** Average response time across WhatsApp, Email, and SMS.
- **Deal Stage Velocity:** Time spent in current pipeline stage.
- **Task & Appointment Completion:** Viewing attendance and follow-up adherence.
