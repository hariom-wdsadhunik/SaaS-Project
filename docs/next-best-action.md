## 1. Action Ranking Algorithm

Each action item is prioritized according to:
- **Priority:** CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW.
- **Urgency:** IMMEDIATE, TODAY, THIS_WEEK.
- **Expected Impact:** Dollar revenue or churn prevention estimate.
- **Reasoning:** Transparent AI motivation.
