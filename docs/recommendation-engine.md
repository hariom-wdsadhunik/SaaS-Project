# LeadPilot AI CRM — AI Recommendation Engine

**Module:** Prescriptive Sales Recommendation Pipeline  
**Version:** v3.7.0  

---

## 1. Recommendation Categories

1. **Contact Lead:** Triggered when engagement propensity peaks.
2. **Schedule Meeting:** Recommended upon viewing document engagement.
3. **Escalate Opportunity:** Recommended for high-value deal bottlenecks.
4. **Move Deal Stage:** Triggered when stage criteria are satisfied.
5. **Re-engage Inactive Lead:** Triggered for idle accounts with historic high scores.
6. **Upsell & Cross-sell Opportunities:** Recommended based on product usage.
