# LeadPilot AI CRM — Release Notes v0.4.0

**Release Date:** July 26, 2026  
**Module:** Customer Management Module (Contacts Domain & Lead Conversion Engine)  

---

## 1. Module Overview

The Contact is the central CRM entity connecting leads, deals, properties, tasks, appointments, and communication histories. In Release v0.4.0, LeadPilot AI CRM introduces a production-grade Customer Management module backed by a live Supabase database repository.

---

## 2. Key Architecture & Features

### A. Production Database Migration ([`supabase/migrations/20260726120000_create_contacts_table.sql`](file:///c:/Users/Hari%20Om%20Kumar/Desktop/GitHub%20Projects/SaaS%20Project/leadpilot-ai/supabase/migrations/20260726120000_create_contacts_table.sql))
- Created `public.contacts` and `public.contact_timeline` tables.
- Implemented strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies for authenticated operations (**no `USING (true)`**).
- Seeded initial high-net-worth real estate contact dataset.

### B. Production Repository ([`src/infrastructure/repositories/SupabaseContactRepository.ts`](file:///c:/Users/Hari%20Om%20Kumar/Desktop/GitHub%20Projects/SaaS%20Project/leadpilot-ai/leadpilot-frontend/src/infrastructure/repositories/SupabaseContactRepository.ts))
- Implements complete `ContactRepository` contract with direct Supabase queries:
  - `getContacts` & `getContactById`
  - `createContact` & `updateContact`
  - `deleteContact` & `deleteContacts`
  - `searchContacts`
  - `favoriteContact` & `archiveContact`
  - `convertLeadToContact`
  - `getTimelineEvents` & `appendTimelineEvent`

### C. Lead Conversion Flow (Lead $\rightarrow$ Contact)
- Converts qualified leads into primary CRM Contacts.
- Preserves complete lead history in `public.leads` table by updating status to `QUALIFIED` / `CONVERTED` without deleting records.
- Automatically appends initial `Lead Created` and `Lead Converted` timeline events to the contact history stream.

### D. Contact Event Append Timeline & Multi-Tag System
- Event stream supports 10 distinct event types: `Lead Created`, `Lead Converted`, `Deal Created`, `Deal Updated`, `Appointment`, `Task`, `Email`, `WhatsApp`, `Notes`, `AI Summary`.
- Supports multi-attribute tags (`VIP`, `Investor`, `Buyer`, `Seller`, `Cold`, `Hot`).

### E. AI Contact Intelligence Tool ([`src/domain/ai/tools/ContactTool.ts`](file:///c:/Users/Hari%20Om%20Kumar/Desktop/GitHub%20Projects/SaaS%20Project/leadpilot-ai/leadpilot-frontend/src/domain/ai/tools/ContactTool.ts))
- Exposes `contact_intelligence_tool` to the AI Assistant enabling deep context analysis across lead history, deals, timeline, notes, and task interactions.
