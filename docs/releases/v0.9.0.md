# LeadPilot AI CRM — Release Notes v0.9.0

**Release Date:** July 26, 2026  
**Module:** Analytics & Business Intelligence Platform Sprint  

---

## 1. Release Overview

Sprint **v0.9.0** delivers an enterprise-grade Analytics & Business Intelligence Platform featuring an 11-metric KPI Engine with TTL caching, custom Report Engine with CSV/Excel/PDF exports, 30-day predictive Forecast Engine, AI Insights Engine, Executive Dashboard service, and versioned `/api/v1/` endpoints.

---

## 2. Key Delivered Features

### A. Analytics Domain & KPI Engine (`src/platform/analytics/`)
- `MetricRegistry.ts`: Registered definitions for 11 core KPIs.
- `MetricCalculator.ts`: Computed conversion rates, win rates, pipeline value, velocity, response time, and activity metrics.
- `MetricCache.ts`: In-memory TTL metric caching.
- `KPIEngine.ts`: High-level facade for KPI calculations and performance status scoring.

### B. Report Engine (`src/platform/analytics/reports/`)
- `ReportBuilder.ts`, `ReportExporter.ts` (CSV, Excel, PDF), `ReportScheduler.ts`, `ReportEngine.ts`.

### C. Forecast Engine (`src/platform/analytics/forecast/`)
- `TrendAnalyzer.ts`, `ForecastModel.ts`, `ForecastEngine.ts` generating 30-day predictive revenue, lead growth, and closure projections.

### D. AI Insights Engine (`src/platform/analytics/insights/`)
- `InsightGenerator.ts`, `InsightRanking.ts`, `InsightEngine.ts` scanning CRM datasets for high-priority leads, slow deals, and inactive customers.

### E. Versioned API Endpoints (`src/app/api/v1/`)
- Versioned endpoints: `/api/v1/analytics`, `/api/v1/reports`, `/api/v1/dashboard`, `/api/v1/forecast`.
