# LeadPilot AI CRM — Release Notes v2.3.0 (Customer Success Platform)

**Release Date:** July 26, 2026  
**Milestone:** Customer Success & Health Score Engine  

---

## Highlights

- **Help Center & Knowledge Base (`src/platform/support/HelpCenterService.ts`)**: Categorized articles, search engine, and article viewer.
- **Support Ticket Engine (`src/platform/support/TicketService.ts`)**: Ticket lifecycle management (`open`, `pending`, `resolved`, `closed`), priorities (`low`, `medium`, `high`, `urgent`), and agent assignment.
- **Customer Health Score Engine (`src/platform/support/HealthScoreEngine.ts`)**: Composite 0-100 telemetry index evaluating login cadence, feature adoption, AI query volume, workflow automation, and support history.
- **API v1 Endpoints**: `/api/v1/help-center`, `/api/v1/feedback`, `/api/v1/tickets`, `/api/v1/customer-health`.
- **Dashboards**: Support & Help Center (`/support`) and Customer Health (`/health`).
