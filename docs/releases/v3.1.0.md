# LeadPilot AI CRM — Release Notes v3.1.0 (AI Sales Copilot Platform)

**Release Date:** July 27, 2026  
**Milestone:** AI Sales Copilot & Proactive Sales Intelligence  

---

## Highlights

- **AI Lead Summary**: Summarizes lead history, extracts key facts, highlights risks & opportunities.
- **AI Email Assistant**: Generates follow-up drafts, rewrites messages, adjusts tone, and summarizes email threads.
- **AI WhatsApp Assistant**: Instant reply drafting, follow-up prompts, and chat summaries.
- **Meeting Preparation Engine**: Generates lead overview, timeline, previous interactions, open tasks, and recommended talking points.
- **Daily AI Brief**: Morning summary of high-priority leads, deals at risk, tasks due today, and suggested actions.
- **Deal Health Engine**: Predicts closing probability, missing information, risk indicators, and next best action.
- **AI Command Center Dashboard**: Integrated dashboard combining morning brief, deal health, action tools, and copilot chat.
