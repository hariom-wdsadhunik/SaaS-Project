# LeadPilot AI CRM — Release Notes v3.3.0 (Enterprise Team Management)

**Release Date:** July 30, 2026  
**Milestone:** Enterprise Multi-User Architecture & Organization Management  

---

## Highlights

- **Organization (Workspace) Model**: Implemented multi-tenant organization boundaries across leads, deals, tasks, properties, appointments, documents, and communications.
- **Team Management Suite**: Member invitation, role assignment, deactivation, online status tracking, and member search.
- **Centralized RBAC Engine**: Implemented 5 roles (`Owner`, `Admin`, `Manager`, `Agent`, `Viewer`) enforcing 6 permissions (`Create`, `Read`, `Update`, `Delete`, `Export`, `Assign`) across 9 domains.
- **Workspace Switcher Component**: Multi-org switcher dropdown with active workspace badge in sidebar.
- **Organization Activity Stream**: Real-time activity timeline tracking user actions across entities.
- **Immutable Security Audit Log**: Compliance log for authentication, RBAC changes, billing events, and data exports.
- **Team UI Pages**:
  - `/team` (Leaderboard & Overview)
  - `/team/members` (Member Management)
  - `/team/invitations` (Pending Invites)
  - `/team/activity` (Activity Stream)
  - `/team/roles` (RBAC Matrix)
  - `/team/audit` (Security Audit Ledger)
- **Documentation**:

  - `docs/rbac.md`
  - `docs/organization-model.md`
  - `docs/audit-log-design.md`
