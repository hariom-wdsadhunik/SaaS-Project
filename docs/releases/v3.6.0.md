# LeadPilot AI CRM — Release Notes v3.6.0 (Enterprise BI & Reporting Platform)

**Release Date:** July 30, 2026  
**Milestone:** Enterprise Business Intelligence & Reporting Platform  

---

## Highlights

- **Centralized Reporting Engine**: Multi-dimensional analytics aggregating Leads, Deals, Tasks, Appointments, Communication, Billing, and AI Copilot data.
- **16-Metric Enterprise KPI Library**: Revenue, Pipeline Value, Deals Won/Lost, Lead Conversion, Sales Velocity, Task Completion, Response Time, MRR, ARR, Churn, and Net Retention.
- **5 Role-Tailored Executive Dashboards**: Executive Leadership, Sales Performance, Financial Intelligence, Operations, and Agent performance.
- **Custom Drag-and-Drop Dashboard Builder**: Customizable widget studio supporting 10 chart types (Line, Bar, Area, Pie, Donut, Funnel, Kanban, Heatmap, Tables, Trend Cards).
- **Automated Report Scheduling**: Daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly email dispatching with PDF/CSV attachments.
- **Multi-Format Data Exporter**: Instant CSV, Excel, PDF, and JSON exports.
- **Reporting UI Sub-App**:
  - `/reports` (Reports Hub & Saved Analytics)
  - `/reports/executive` (Executive Leadership Brief)
  - `/reports/sales` (Sales Performance & Funnel)
  - `/reports/finance` (Financial Analytics & MRR/ARR)
  - `/reports/custom` (Custom Dashboard Builder)
  - `/reports/scheduled` (Scheduled Report Delivery)
