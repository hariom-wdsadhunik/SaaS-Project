# LeadPilot AI CRM — Release Notes v3.7.0 (Enterprise AI Intelligence Platform)

**Release Date:** July 30, 2026  
**Milestone:** Enterprise AI Intelligence Platform  

---

## Highlights

- **Centralized AI Intelligence Engine (`AiIntelligenceEngine.ts`)**: Predictive ML engine providing Lead Scoring, Opportunity Win Probability, Churn Risk, Revenue Forecasting, and Next Best Action recommendations.
- **Predictive Lead Scoring Console**: Propensity scores (0-100), grades (A-D), confidence levels, and feature importance weighting.
- **AI Revenue Forecasting**: Monthly, quarterly, and annual projections with Monte Carlo simulations, best/worst case scenarios, and 85-95% confidence intervals.
- **Next Best Action Engine**: Prescriptive sales guidance prioritizing critical follow-ups based on expected revenue impact and urgency.
- **Explainable AI (XAI)**: Transparent reasoning breakdowns for all predictions showing positive and negative feature drivers.
- **AI Platform UI Sub-App**:
  - `/ai` (AI Command Center Overview)
  - `/ai/insights` (Explainable AI Insights & Factors)
  - `/ai/forecast` (Predictive Revenue Forecasting & Bounds)
  - `/ai/leads` (Predictive Lead & Deal Scoring Console)
  - `/ai/recommendations` (Next Best Action Recommendation Hub)
