# LeadPilot AI CRM — Reporting Engine Specification

**Module:** Enterprise Business Intelligence & Reporting Platform  
**Version:** v3.6.0  

---

## 1. Overview

Sprint **v3.6.0** introduces a centralized reporting engine aggregating CRM metrics across leads, deals, tasks, appointments, omnichannel communications, billing subscriptions, and AI Copilot activities.

---

## 2. Capabilities

- **Report Definitions & Saved Reports:** Filter, group, and aggregate CRM entities into reusable definitions.
- **Dynamic Drill-Down:** Detailed record inspection directly from visual chart components.
- **Export Multi-Format Engine:** Instant generation of CSV, Excel, PDF, and JSON reports.
