# LeadPilot AI CRM — Strategic v4.0.0 Architecture & Roadmap Assessment

**Version:** v3.8.0  
**Target Milestone:** v4.0.0 (Global Enterprise Cloud & Autonomous Agents)  

---

## 1. Vision for v4.0.0

Having completed the v3 milestone with **Sprint v3.8.0 Enterprise Administration & Operations Platform**, LeadPilot AI CRM is positioned for enterprise expansion in v4.0.0.

---

## 2. Recommended v4.0.0 Strategic Initiatives

1. **Autonomous Sales Agent Swarms:** Multi-agent role-based orchestration capable of auto-negotiating meeting slots and conducting initial discovery interviews.
2. **GraphQL & gRPC Federation:** High-throughput streaming API endpoints for enterprise ERP integrations (SAP, Salesforce, Dynamics 365).
3. **Multi-Region Database Replication:** Active-active Supabase Postgres replication across US, EU, and APAC availability zones.
