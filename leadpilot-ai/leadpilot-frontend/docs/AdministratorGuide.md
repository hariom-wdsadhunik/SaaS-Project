# LeadPilot AI CRM — Administrator Guide

## Organization Management

- **Tenant Boundaries:** Manage team members and roles (`ADMIN`, `MANAGER`, `BROKER`, `AGENT`, `VIEWER`).
- **Workflow Automation:** Configure automated trigger/action rules via `WorkflowEngine`.
- **System Health:** Access health and performance metrics via `/api/v1/health` and `AuditDashboard`.
