# Iconography & Illustration System Guidelines

**Module:** Iconography & Illustration Direction  
**Version:** v2.0.0  

---

## 1. Iconography Standards

LeadPilot AI CRM uses `lucide-react` line icons at 1.5px stroke weight.

- **Navigation Icons:** LayoutDashboard, Users, Briefcase, Calendar, CheckSquare, MessageSquare, Folder, Bot.
- **CRM & Analytics Icons:** TrendingUp, DollarSign, Activity, FileText, CheckCircle2, ShieldAlert.

---

## 2. Illustration & Empty State Direction

1. **Empty States:** Clean 2D outline vectors with subtle glowing accent backdrops.
2. **404 & Error States:** Minimalist technical geometric graphics.
3. **Success States:** Vibrant emerald badges with checkmark feedback.
